A chance left blank uses the default shown in grey. % of all ride screens is the chance a character drops in on any ride; % on their own rides is the chance on the rides in the Rides list: press Add to see every park's rides side by side and tick the ones the character should visit (the rides a character already visits are ticked). The Total at the bottom adds up the first column (the defaults come to 12.9%) and can't pass 100%. A character you untick is replaced by the plain wipe, not by another character, so the ticked characters' total is how often a surprise really plays.
